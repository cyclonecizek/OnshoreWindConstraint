Constraint

NO-GO when the 10-second average wind at 200 ft exceeds the limit, from any direction.

Sources

    Sustained is each member's wind at 200 ft (the model wind moved up from 10 m and 80 m, as on the Wind at 200 ft page). Gust is the estimated peak at 200 ft: the sustained wind at 200 ft plus the model's 10 m gust excess. Both are hour-by-hour model values, not observations.

    10-second wind is an estimate of the highest 10-second average in the hour: sustained + factor × (gust − sustained). Standard gust-factor conversions between averaging times put a 10-second peak about three quarters of the way from the mean wind to the 3-second gust, over both sea and land, so the default factor is 0.75; it can be changed under Constraint. Chances are the weighted share of members above the limit, with each source's weight split across its members.
