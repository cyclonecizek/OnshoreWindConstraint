    Each member is classed go, assessment or no-go on magnitude and on direction separately; its combined class is the lower of the two. Probability of violation is the weighted share of members in a class. Each source's weight is split across its members that have data at that hour, so large global ensembles do not outvote the hi-res guidance by member count.

    Winds are moved to the evaluation height by interpolating u and v in ln(height) between the 10 m and 80+ m model levels, or by power-law scaling when only one level exists. Gust at height is the mean wind at height plus the 10 m gust excess. HRRR and RRFS are time-lagged ensembles of recent cycles; REFS and HREF members are drawn from each system's published mean and spread.
